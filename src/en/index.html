The method

Three phases. Ninety days. A machine that runs on its own at the end.

  1. 01

    Weeks 1–2

    Phase 1: Recruiting

    We design the full org chart and run recruiting end to end: behavioral profile testing, screening and validation. You only interview a list that has already been filtered.

  2. 02

    Weeks 3–4

    Phase 2: Installation

    We install the system: CRM, management tools and recorded playbooks for running a campaign, publishing content and answering a lead. It is a course inside your own company.

  3. 03

    Weeks 5–12

    Phase 3: Governance

    Instead of handing over a manual and leaving, we spend eight weeks managing the new team. We hold them to delivery and review targets, cost per lead, KPIs and OKRs, until the machine keeps running on its own.
