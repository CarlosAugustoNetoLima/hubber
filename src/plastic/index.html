The problem

Most practices don’t lose patients to a competitor. They lose them along the way.

  1. 01Discovery

    A potential patient sees a reel or an ad. If it looks like everyone else’s, they keep scrolling.

  2. 02Trust

    They check your profile, your results and your reviews. Gaps here cost more than a weak ad.

  3. 03Contact

    They send a message or fill out a form. If the reply takes a day, they may already be talking to another practice.

  4. 04Consultation

    Someone books. Nobody knows which campaign, post or page brought them, so the budget keeps going to guesses.

When content, ads, website and follow-up are run by people who never talk to each other, each one can look fine on its own report while opportunities fall through the gaps.
