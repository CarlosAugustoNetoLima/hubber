Case studies · Build · Car rental · Orlando

Florida Rental Car

The largest car rental company in Orlando did not want one more agency to run ads. It wanted the marketing team structured from the inside. This is the client that made Hubber stop selling services and start building structure.

Product: Hubber Build Market: United States

Context

A company already selling, and still unable to grow with control.

A car rental business in Orlando, operating in the United States, with real demand and established revenue. The problem was never a lack of customers.

  • Starting point

    Marketing entirely outsourced, with nobody inside the company responsible for thinking about acquisition.

  • Dominant constraint

    Structure. What was missing was a team, processes and commercial visibility, not demand.

  • Why Build

    What the client was asking for was capability of his own. Hiring more external execution would have postponed the same problem.

The intervention

What Hubber implemented.

Four fronts, in the order where each one holds up the next.

  1. 01

    Front 1: Team

    Recruiting from scratch: designing the roles, selecting and hiring the people who would answer for marketing inside the company.

  2. 02

    Front 2: System

    CRM implemented and configured, with the pipeline visible from lead to after-sales. Before that there was nowhere to look to know what was happening in the sale.

  3. 03

    Front 3: Training

    Training the new team on the routines that would become theirs, so the operation would not depend on whoever built it.

  4. 04

    Front 4: Processes

    Marketing and sales processes designed end to end, including after-sales, which is where most operations stop measuring.
