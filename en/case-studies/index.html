The bar

What has to exist before a case study goes on this page.

Publishing a case study is easy. Publishing one that survives a question is another matter.

  • Written permission from the client to publish.
  • A starting point measured before the intervention, not estimated afterwards.
  • A declared period, with a start and an end.
  • A number with a source, and what can and cannot be attributed to Hubber.

Until a case study has all four, it stays off this page. That is why there are fewer case studies here than there are clients.
