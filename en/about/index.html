The thesis

The thesis did not come from a book. It came from a client who asked for something else.

Fabinho, who owns the largest car rental company in Orlando, did not want to hire one more agency to run ads. He wanted us to structure and professionalize his marketing team from the inside.

We did: CRM, recruiting from scratch, training, and marketing and sales processes through to after-sales. And we realized that was not a service outside the catalogue. It was the catalogue. That is where Hubber stopped selling services.

How we work

Six rules that matter more than any proposal.

  • Diagnosis before solution

    Nobody gets a proposal before the constraint is confirmed in writing.

  • Price after the diagnostic

    A number at the end of the conversation, never before understanding what the company needs.

  • A readiness gate

    If the conditions for execution are not there, we say so in the diagnostic and we do not start.

  • Scope in writing

    What is in, what is out and what sits on each side, before signing.

  • Everything in the client's name

    Accounts, tools, playbooks and team stay with the company, not with Hubber.

  • No guaranteed numbers

    We guarantee scope, method and transparency. Results depend on both sides.
